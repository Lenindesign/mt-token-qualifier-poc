==============================================================================

1. SCALE — this is a restructure, not a rename

  Ignition   54 tokens, one flat tier of numbered ramps
  Hearst     3035 tokens, three tiers named by role
  Ratio      ~56x more granular

  Ignition names colours by POSITION (primary-2, neutral-4).
  Hearst names them by ROLE (bg-brand, txt-default, border-subtle).
  So one IDS token fans out into many HDS roles, and most HDS tokens
  have no IDS ancestor at all. There is no 1:1 mapping to write.

2. BRAND REACH — how much of HDS MotorTrend actually controls

  Brand hooks in HDS               14
  Tokens reachable through them    56 of 3035  (1.8%)

  Injecting MotorTrend's palette into HDS reaches 1.8% of its tokens.
  The other 2979 are fixed: HDS's own neutrals, status colours and
  component tokens. Migrating means accepting those, or negotiating
  overrides that HDS has no mechanism for today.

4. CATEGORY COVERAGE — what has to be created rather than mapped

  color            30 / 599  ██
  typography        0 / 574  █   ← IDS typography is utility classes, not tokens
  spacing           0 / 12  █   ← IDS has no spacing tokens at all
  component         0 / 1458  █   ← IDS has no component tier

  Typography, spacing and component tokens are not migrations. They are
  net-new design work: 0 → 2044 tokens with no Ignition ancestor.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE MIGRATION PLAN

  · A token-for-token mapping table is the wrong artifact. The work is
    re-expressing MotorTrend intent in HDS role vocabulary.
  · Only 1.8% of HDS is brand-controllable, so the first decision is
    whether MotorTrend accepts HDS's neutrals and status colours as-is.
  · Typography, spacing and component tiers are new design work, not
    ports. Budget for design time, not just engineering time.
  · Breakpoints must be agreed before any component moves.
